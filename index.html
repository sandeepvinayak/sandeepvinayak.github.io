Hello, I’m Sandeep👋

A seasoned engineering leader with over a decade of experience at top technology companies including Salesforce, Snapchat, Microsoft, and Intel. Currently, I work as a Principal Engineer at Salesforce in San Francisco, where I lead multi-cloud development standards and strategy to enable Salesforce on AWS, GCP, and Alibaba with defined standards and best practices.

I am also a founding member of MultiCloudJ, our open-source SDK for hybrid and multi-cloud development, and have successfully contributed to several other open source projects such as Apache HBase, Apache Phoenix, GoCloud, and Apache Spark. My expertise spans distributed systems, large-scale databases, identity and access management (IAM), and cloud-native platforms. I focus on designing resilient architectures, driving technical strategy, and mentoring teams to deliver secure, scalable, and enterprise-grade solutions. I am deeply curious about engineering challenges, complex problem-solving, and building systems that can scale from thousands to millions of users while maintaining performance and reliability.
